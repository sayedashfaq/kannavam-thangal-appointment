Privacy Policy

Kannavam Thangal Appointment System
Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kannavam Thangal Appointment System (“we”, “our”, “the Service”) collects and uses information when you book or manage appointments through WhatsApp.

1. Information we collect

When you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your place / locality
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your WhatsApp number (if different)
  • Appointment details such as token number, consultation day, location, reporting time, and booking status
  • Message content needed to complete the booking conversation

2. How we use your information

We use this information only to:

  • Create and manage appointment tokens
  • Send booking confirmations and related WhatsApp messages
  • Notify the authorised administrator of new bookings
  • Prevent duplicate bookings and enforce daily token limits
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Service

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, to send and receive messages
  • Our hosting and database providers, solely to run the Service
  • The authorised administrator of the consultation service

4. Data retention

Booking records are kept for as long as needed for consultation management, record-keeping, and support. Conversation progress data may be cleared after a booking is completed or cancelled.

5. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your choices and deletion

To request correction or deletion of your booking data, contact us using the details below. You may also ask the administrator via WhatsApp to cancel a booking.

Data deletion instructions: https://kannavam-thangal-appointment.onrender.com/data-deletion

7. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for adults arranging consultations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: mrthangupay@gmail.com
