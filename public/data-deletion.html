Data Deletion Instructions

Kannavam Thangal Appointment System
Last updated: 26 July 2026

If you want your appointment or personal data removed from the Kannavam Thangal Appointment System, follow the steps below.

1. Request deletion by email

Send an email to mrthangupay@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Include:

  • Your full name
  • The mobile number used for booking
  • The booking date or token number, if known

2. Request deletion via WhatsApp

Message the appointment WhatsApp number from the same phone used for booking and ask for your data to be deleted. The authorised administrator may cancel your booking and arrange removal of related records.

3. What we delete

Upon a verified request, we will delete or anonymise:

  • Your booking records
  • Stored conversation progress linked to your phone number
  • Related contact details used for the appointment

4. Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some technical logs may be retained for a limited period for security and operational reasons.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: mrthangupay@gmail.com
